## What B1 is for

B1 is where the grammar already met is put under pressure rather than extended
indefinitely. Aspect returns as the level's spine — this time in the future, in
the infinitive, in the imperative and in negation, where the choice is least
intuitive. The vocative, introduced as a form at A1, becomes a matter of
politeness and relationship. And the reading diet changes: from A1 and A2 texts
written for the course to openly licensed and public-domain Ukrainian sources,
cited in full.

## Sources

B1 is the first level that reads authentic material, and the sourcing rule is
absolute: **public-domain or openly licensed text only**, quoted with author,
title, source and licence, and never reproduced beyond what the task needs.
Where a topic — particularly a historical one — cannot be treated from open
sources without distortion, it is summarised in the course's own words and the
sources are linked rather than reproduced.

## Assessment and conformance

Eighteen sibling unit exams plus a **B1 level exam**, with the PDF committed
under `static/downloads/b1/`. Completing B1 completes the course's declared
`core` conformance target: every in-scope curriculum scale with an A1, A2 or B1
descriptor is either implemented or explicitly recorded as having no official
descriptor at that level. B2 and C1 are declared gaps, not silent omissions.
