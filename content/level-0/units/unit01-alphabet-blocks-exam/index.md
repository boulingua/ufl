{{< callout type="warning" title="Level 0 · Unit 1 exam — 30 minutes, 40 points" >}}
**Shape:** Task → Model → Strategy → Attempt → Feedback.\
This is a **decoding** test, not a language test. Nothing is asked about
meaning that the unit has not taught, and nothing is asked about grammar at
all. No transliteration is given anywhere in the tasks.
{{< /callout >}}

## Task 1 — Letter recognition (10 points)

Give the sound value of each letter, and say which block it belongs to
(1 free · 2 dangerous · 3 drill · 4 new job).

`В` · `Ї` · `Ц` · `О` · `Х` · `Ґ` · `И` · `Р` · `Щ` · `Ь`

## Task 2 — Read aloud (10 points)

Read the six words aloud. One point for the consonants, one for the vowels, in
each word. The examiner marks a word wrong if a single letter is read with a
Latin value.

`курс` · `ка́ва` · `метро́` · `хліб` · `цу́кор` · `сувені́р`

## Task 3 — The four Ukrainian letters (8 points)

Complete each sentence with `ґ`, `і`, `ї` or `є`, and say why.

1. The word for *forest* is `л__с`; the word for *fox* is `лис`.
2. The word for *porch* begins with a hard *g*: `__а́нок`.
3. The name of the country contains a letter that says *yi*: `Укра__на`.
4. The name of the continent begins with *ye*: `__вро́па`.

## Task 4 — Signs (6 points)

Read each sign aloud and say, in English or German, what kind of place it is.

`ПО́ШТА` · `ТЕА́ТР` · `ШКО́ЛА`

## Task 5 — Write what you hear (6 points)

The examiner reads each item twice, at normal speed. Write it in Cyrillic.

1. *Oksana* 2. *Marko* 3. *Lviv*

{{< details title="Answer key and marking" type="tip" >}}
**Task 1 (10).** `В` v, block 2 · `Ї` yi, block 4 · `Ц` ts, block 3 ·
`О` o, block 1 · `Х` kh, block 2 · `Ґ` hard g, block 4 · `И` /ɪ/, block 3 ·
`Р` trilled r, block 2 · `Щ` shch, block 4 · `Ь` no sound, softens the
preceding letter, block 4. One point per letter; the sound value alone earns
the point, the block is a diagnostic note for the teacher.

**Task 2 (10).** *kurs · kava · metro · khlib · tsukor · suvenir.* Deduct the
whole word for any Latin-value reading — *kaba*, *metpo*, *cypop* — and note
which block the error came from.

**Task 3 (8).** 1. `ліс` — `і` is /i/, `и` is /ɪ/, and the two words are a
minimal pair. 2. `ґа́нок` — `ґ` is the hard *g*; `г` would give /ɦ/.
3. `Украї́на` — `ї` is *yi*, two sounds. 4. `Євро́па` — `є` carries the /j/
that `е` lacks. Two points each: one for the letter, one for the reason.

**Task 4 (6).** *poshta* — post office · *teatr* — theatre · *shkola* —
school. Two points each: one for reading, one for identifying.

**Task 5 (6).** `Окса́на` · `Ма́рко` · `Льві́в`. Two points each. Accept the
words without stress marks — stress is not written in ordinary Ukrainian.
Do **not** accept `Львив` or `Лвів`: the `і` and the soft sign both count.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details title="Grading scale (out of 40)" type="tip" >}}
| 37–40 | 1 | 33–36 | 2 | 28–32 | 3 |
| 24–27 | 4 | 16–23 | 5 | 0–15 | 6 |

The pass mark is 24 (60 %). A learner below 32 should repeat Unit 1 before
starting Unit 2: everything that follows assumes fluent decoding, and a
decoding gap does not close on its own.
{{< /details >}}

<div class="notes">
**Administration.** 30 minutes. Tasks 2 and 4 are spoken and are taken
individually or in pairs while the rest of the group works on Tasks 1, 3 and 5.
Task 5 needs a reader; the three items are read twice at normal speed with a
pause between them.

**Differentiation — support.** Allow the block table from the unit page during
Task 1. Reduce Task 2 to four words, keeping `ка́ва` and `метро́` — the two that
diagnose the dangerous block.

**Extension.** After Task 5, ask for the learner's own first and last name in
Cyrillic, and for one sentence in English or German explaining which letter of
their name was the hardest to choose and why.
</div>
