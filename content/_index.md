## What this is

An open course in Ukrainian as a foreign language, written by one teacher and
published in full: units, model exams, editable slide decks, printable
worksheets and native-voice audio. It is aimed at adults and at secondary
pupils in the German *Gesamtschule* system, and it assumes **no prior
Cyrillic** — not as a courtesy, but as a design constraint that shapes the
first six units.

Every unit follows the same five-move shape — **Activate → Input → Practise →
Apply → Reflect** — so a teacher can plan a double lesson from the page and a
self-study learner always knows where they are.

## Why Level 0 exists

Most beginner courses start with *hello, my name is*. In Ukrainian that
sentence is unreadable on day one, because reading itself is a skill the
learner does not yet have. Level 0 treats decoding as a taught competence
rather than an assumption:

- the alphabet in **blocks** — letters that look and sound like Latin ones,
  letters that look familiar and lie, and letters that are new outright;
- the specifically Ukrainian letters **`ґ  і  ї  є`**, drilled against their
  Russian look-alikes, because most interference errors start here;
- **stress**, which is mobile and meaningful, marked in every glossed word
  through A1;
- the **apostrophe** and the **soft sign**, which change pronunciation and are
  routinely mistyped;
- **typing** — a learner who cannot produce `ґ` or `'` cannot do the homework.

Through Level 0 and early A1 every Ukrainian word is glossed with a Latin
transliteration and a pronunciation hint; the scaffolding is withdrawn in
stages and is gone by the end of A1.

## The language this course teaches

Standard literary Ukrainian, following the 2019 «Український правопис». No
dialect coverage: surzhyk and regional variation appear as intercultural
asides, clearly labelled, not as models to imitate. Where the 2019 norm
differs from older forms learners will meet in print, the difference is noted
rather than hidden.

## Materials, exams and audio

Each unit ships with an editable **.odp** slide deck and a printable **PDF**
worksheet, generated from the shared branded templates and committed to the
repository. Each unit has a sibling **model exam** with duration, points and a
grading scale; each level has a level exam. Dialogues and reading texts are
recorded with a genuinely Ukrainian synthetic voice — never a Russian
fallback — and every recording is paired with its transcript on the page.

## Status

The site structure, the level plan and the legal and licensing scaffold are in
place. Content lands level by level: Level 0 and A1 first — the public launch
tranche — then A2 and B1. Planned units are listed on each level page so the
shape of the finished course is visible before it is finished.
