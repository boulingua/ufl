## The recurring cast

The same three people carry the dialogues, reading texts and audio from Level 0
through B1, so that vocabulary and relationships accumulate instead of resetting
every unit:

- **Оксана** — a student in Lviv. She is the point-of-view character for most
  A1 dialogues: her flat, her timetable, her shopping, her weather complaints.
- **Марко** — her cousin, living abroad. He supplies the reasons to write, to
  phone, and to explain what things are called.
- **пані Коваленко** — a teacher. She is the formal register in the room: the
  reason `Ви` exists, and the source of instructions and corrections.

## What A1 covers

Grammar at A1 is heavier in Ukrainian than in a Germanic or Romance course,
because nothing can be said without inflecting it. The load is spread
deliberately: gender and number first, then the nominative as the citation form,
then the accusative as the first case with real work to do, and only then the
present tense of the two conjugations. Aspect is named at A1 but not exploited;
it is A2's subject.

## Assessment

Every unit has a sibling model exam of its own, and the level closes with an
**A1 level exam** covering all four skills, with a stated duration, a total
point score and a published grading scale. Exam PDFs are committed to the
repository under `static/downloads/a1/` and linked from the exam pages.

## Materials and audio

Each unit ships an editable slide deck and a printable worksheet, plus audio
for the dialogue and the vocabulary list. Audio is recorded with a Ukrainian
voice and paired with an on-page transcript; there is no Russian fallback voice
anywhere in this course.
