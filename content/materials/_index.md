Downloadable classroom materials for this course. Slide decks (editable `.odp`
with a PDF alongside) and worksheets (print-ready PDF) are generated from the
shared branded templates in the course's signature colour and are **committed**
to the repository under `static/materials/`; the build only verifies that every
referenced file exists and is correctly attributed.

Ukrainian sets one extra requirement on the toolchain: the PDFs are typeset
with a Unicode engine and a font with full Ukrainian Cyrillic coverage, so that
`ґ`, `ї`, `є` and the apostrophe survive the trip to paper. A worksheet that
prints `ґ` as a box is a defect, not a cosmetic issue — please report it.

Audio lives with its unit rather than here: each recording sits beside its
transcript on the unit page.

This page is a navigation surface and carries no counting mark.
