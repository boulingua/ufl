## In preparation

- **The seven cases** — full declension tables for nouns, adjectives and
  pronouns, with the endings that A1 and A2 units introduce piecemeal set out
  as one system.
- **Verb aspect pairs** — the high-frequency imperfective/perfective pairs of
  A2 and B1, grouped by the relation between the members rather than
  alphabetically.
- **Numbers, time and dates** — cardinals, ordinals, the case a number governs,
  the clock, and the calendar.
- **Ukrainian and German: false friends and interference** — including the
  errors that specifically arise from prior Russian, which is the single most
  common source of interference among this course's learners.
- **Intercultural notes** — register, forms of address, the vocative in social
  use, and a plain statement of what this course does and does not say about
  regional variation and surzhyk.
- **Assessment grid** — the criteria used to mark productive tasks and exams
  at each level, so that a grade can be explained rather than merely awarded.

Each appendix is a page bundle under `content/appendices/`, follows the same
front-matter core as a unit, and — being original long-form prose — carries its
own statutory counting mark once registered.
